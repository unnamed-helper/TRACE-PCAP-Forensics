# Community Code of Conduct

We want TRACE to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Welcome different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, and personal attacks.
- Keep security reports private until maintainers coordinate disclosure.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
